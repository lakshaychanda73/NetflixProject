## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### 1.1 The one-paragraph version

A US developer-operator (Hines) and a Japanese trading house (Sumitomo Corporation) are building a
lake-adjacent, low-density luxury condominium on a ~3.2–3.4 acre freehold parcel in Chandivali/Powai,
roughly 50 m off the Jogeshwari–Vikhroli Link Road. Public record puts the JV at **Sumitomo 90% /
Hines 10%**, held through an Indian SPV, with ~**3.82 lakh sq ft** of saleable area. The sales team is
marketing 3 towers / 17 habitable floors / ~250 residences of 3 & 4 BHK at ~**₹55,000 per sq ft**,
possession December 2030. At that rate the product sits **10–25% above the Powai/Hiranandani resale

value trade. The differentiators offered (the "Butterfly" plate, 200 sq ft floating decks, a 23 ft
lobby, 2.42 acres of green) are real design choices with real cost behind them, but two of the three
are **unverified against a sanctioned plan**, and the single most important commercial fact — the
RERA registration — **did not exist in the public record at the time of writing**. Nothing here should
be represented to a buyer as fact until the RERA filing lands.

### 3.3 The area ladder — and the question that decides everything

Three different "areas" exist for every Mumbai apartment. Sales teams move between them. You must not.

```
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  RERA CARPET AREA          The net usable floor area within walls.       │
 │  (the only legal number)   Excludes external walls, shafts, balcony,     │
 │                            terrace. THIS is what s.2(k) RERA defines,    │
 │                            and what must appear in the Agreement.        │
 ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │  CARPET + BALCONY/DECK     Marketing convenience. Legal only if the      │
 │                            agreement states both separately.             │
 ├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
 │  SALEABLE / SUPER BUILT-UP Carpet grossed up for common areas.           │
 │                            Typically ×1.35–1.55 in Mumbai luxury.        │
 │                            NOT a RERA concept. Cannot be the price base  │
 │                            in the Agreement for Sale.                    │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Why this is the whole ballgame:**

```
  If 1,120 sq ft is RERA CARPET and ₹55,000 is on carpet:
      agreement value ≈ ₹6.16 Cr  → effective ₹55,000 / carpet sq ft
  If 1,120 sq ft is CARPET and ₹55,000 is on SALEABLE (×1.45 = 1,624 sq ft):
      agreement value ≈ ₹8.93 Cr  → effective ₹79,750 / carpet sq ft   ← +45%
  If 1,120 sq ft is SALEABLE and ₹55,000 is on saleable (carpet ≈ 772 sq ft):
      agreement value ≈ ₹6.16 Cr  → effective ₹79,700 / carpet sq ft   ← +45%

  ⇒ The SAME two numbers produce a 45% swing in the only metric a buyer
    can compare across projects. Get it in writing. Part 10, Question 1.
```

areas quoted are **RERA carpet**, balconies stated separately, ₹55,000 applied to carpet. `[E]`

### 3.4 Decoding the "Butterfly Structure"  ·  *engineering detail in 7B.5*

Your sketch shows three plates around a central spine with wings splaying outward. That is a
**pinwheel / butterfly plate** — a well-established high-rise typology. Here is what it actually buys,
and what it costs.
