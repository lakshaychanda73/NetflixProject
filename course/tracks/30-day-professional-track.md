# 30-Day Professional Track

**Assumes 2–3 hours a day.** This is the full programme: certification **plus** the practical,
market and financial depth that makes you credible with developers, investors and institutional
buyers — not just compliant.

Structure: **Week 1** law · **Week 2** application · **Week 3** money and market · **Week 4**
synthesis and mastery.

### Day 30 · Mock exam + integration
✍️ Re-do the questions you got wrong. Then answer these five out loud, as if to a developer:
1. *"What's the difference between RERA and MahaRERA?"*
2. *"Take me through how you'd check my project before recommending it."*
3. *"What's the all-in cost of a ₹2 crore flat in Goregaon?"*
4. *"Where's MMR growth coming from and why?"*
5. *"Why won't you sell my pre-launch?"*
🧠 If you can answer all five fluently, you're ready — for the exam **and** the room.
