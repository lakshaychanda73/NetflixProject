# 14-Day Fast Track — MahaRERA Certification Focus

**Assumes 2–3 hours a day.** Optimised for **passing the exam**, with just enough practical work
to stop it being purely theoretical. If you have a month, use the
[30-Day Professional Track](30-day-professional-track.md) instead.

**Exam reminder:** 50 MCQs × 2 marks = 100; pass mark **40**; Marathi or English. ⏱ Verify at
[MahaRERA](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/Orders_and_circulars/120.pdf).

## What you will and won't have after 14 days

**You will have:** the exam covered, one real project investigated end to end, a cost-sheet
calculator, and a marked-up agreement.

**You won't have:** Mumbai market depth (Module 11), developer finance (Module 12) or research
skill (Module 13). Those are the modules that make you *good*, as opposed to *certified*.
**Come back for them after the exam** — the 30-day track sequences them properly.
