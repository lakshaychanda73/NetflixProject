## 2. Simple explanation

### 2.1 Who is a real estate agent? (syllabus 5.1) ⚖️

**Section 2(zm)** — a real estate agent is a person who **negotiates or acts on behalf of one
person in a transaction of transfer** of a plot, apartment or building in a real estate project,
**and receives remuneration, fees or any other charge** for their services. It expressly includes
property dealers, brokers and middlemen by whatever name called.

Two tests, both must be present:
1. You **negotiate or act for another person** in a transfer, **and**
2. You receive **consideration** for it.

**Therefore:**
- Introducing a buyer to a developer for a commission → **agent**.
- Being a salaried employee of the promoter selling its own project → **not** an agent (you're the promoter's staff).
- "Channel partner", "sourcing partner", "associate", "consultant" → **agent**, whatever the contract calls it. The Act says *by whatever name called*.
- Doing one deal as a favour, with a "gift" afterwards → treat as **agent**. Don't test this.

### 2.2 Registration under Section 9 (syllabus 5.2) ⚖️

**s.9(1):** No real estate agent shall facilitate the sale or purchase of, or act on behalf of any
person in, any plot/apartment/building **in a real estate project registered under this Act**
without obtaining registration.

Note the precision: the obligation attaches to dealing in a **registered project**. Read together
with **s.10(a)** — which forbids facilitating a transaction in a project that *required*
registration but doesn't have it — the combined effect is simple and total: **you need to be
registered, and you may only deal in projects that are registered.**

**s.9(6):** registration may be **revoked** or suspended after an opportunity of being heard.

### 2.3 Types of agent (syllabus 5.3)

| Type | Who | Fee treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | A single natural person | Individual fee |
| **Sole proprietorship** | One person trading under a firm name | Treated as **individual** for fees |
| **Partnership firm** | Two or more partners | Non-individual |
| **LLP** | Limited liability partnership | Non-individual |
| **Private / Public limited company** | Incorporated entity | Non-individual |
| **Society / Trust** | Registered body | Non-individual |

⏱ **Fees (Maharashtra) — verify before paying:**
**₹10,000 + GST** for an individual (including sole proprietorship) and **₹1,00,000 + GST** for a
non-individual. **Validity: 5 years** unless revoked. The same fee structure applies on renewal.
*These come from the Maharashtra Rules, 2017 and are subject to change — always confirm on the
portal before you pay.*

### 2.4 Registration application & process (syllabus 5.4)

The process is **entirely online**.

1. Create an **agent account** on **https://maharerait.mahaonline.gov.in/**
2. Log in; complete each section of the agent registration form.
3. Upload the mandatory documents.
4. Accept the declaration.
5. Pay online.
6. An **application number** is generated — track scrutiny status against it.
7. On approval, you receive a **registration certificate with a unique registration number**, valid 5 years.

**Documents typically required** (confirm the current list on the portal):

| Individual | Non-individual |
|---|---|
| PAN card | PAN of the entity |
| Aadhaar / address proof | Registration/incorporation certificate |
| Photograph | MOA & AOA / partnership deed / LLP agreement |
| Address proof of place of business | Address proof of registered office |
| **Income tax returns — last 3 financial years** | ITRs — last 3 financial years |
| Details of any **pending civil or criminal cases** | Same, for the entity and its officers |
| Letterhead / rubber stamp specimen | Letterhead / rubber stamp specimen; authorised signatory details |
| **Certificate of Competency** (training & certification) | Certificate of Competency for the authorised person(s) |

> ⚠️ The declaration about **pending civil and criminal cases** is a live obligation, not a
> formality. A non-disclosure discovered later is a much bigger problem than the disclosure itself.

### 2.5 Training and Certificate of Competency 🔴

MahaRERA made **training plus a certification examination** a condition for agent registration and
renewal — the reason the MATP exists. From the official guidelines:

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| Foundational order | **Order No. 41 dated 10.01.2023** made certification mandatory for registration/renewal |
| Guidelines order | **Order No. 47/2023, dated 2 June 2023** |
| Guidelines document | [MahaRERA `120.pdf`](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/Orders_and_circulars/120.pdf) |
| Exam format | Multiple choice, four options per question |
| Questions / marks | **50 questions × 2 marks = 100 marks** (as per the guidelines) |
| Passing marks | **40 out of 100** |
| Exam fee cap | Not exceeding **₹1,500** (excluding GST) |
| Outcome | **Certificate of Competency** with a unique certification number |
| Language | Marathi or English |

⏱ **Time-sensitive.** Deadlines were extended more than once (new agents' deadline moved from
1 Sept 2023 to 1 Nov 2023; existing agents to 1 Jan 2024) and the operating detail may have moved
again. **Confirm the current exam pattern and fee on the MahaRERA site before your exam.**

### 2.6 Renewal (syllabus 5.5)

- Registration runs **5 years** from grant.
- Apply through the **Renewal** tab in your agent account **before expiry**.
- Renewal requires updated documents and a **valid Certificate of Competency**.
- **Do not let it lapse.** Dealing after expiry is dealing without registration — s.9 breach,
  s.62 penalty at **₹10,000 per day**.

💼 **Practice tip:** set a calendar reminder at **4 years 9 months**. Renewal is administrative;
lapse is expensive.

### 2.7 Functions and duties of agents (syllabus 5.6) ⚖️ — Section 10

A registered agent **shall not**:
- **(a)** facilitate the sale/purchase of any plot/apartment/building in a project **not registered** under the Act where registration is required;
- **(c)** involve themselves in any **unfair trade practice**, including:
  - making any statement, orally or in writing, which **misrepresents** the services, standard, quality or grade of the project;
  - representing that the promoter or agent has approvals or affiliations they do not have;
  - making a **false or misleading representation** about the services;
  - permitting the publication of an advertisement of **services that are not intended to be offered**.

A registered agent **shall**:
- **(b)** maintain and preserve **books of account, records and documents** as prescribed;
- **(d)** **facilitate the possession of all information and documents** to which the allottee is entitled at the time of booking;
- **(e)** discharge such other functions as may be prescribed.

**Translate that into working behaviour:**

| The law says | In your day this means |
|---|---|
| No unregistered projects | Check the MahaRERA number **before** the first site visit, not before the booking |
| Maintain books | Keep a record of every deal: parties, project, amounts, invoices, brokerage, correspondence |
| No misrepresentation | Never quote possession dates, amenities or approvals that aren't in the disclosure |
| No false advertising | Every ad carries the registration number, website and QR per Order 46C/2025 |
| Facilitate documents | Give the buyer the disclosure, the proforma agreement and the cost sheet — proactively |

### 2.8 Revocation (syllabus 5.7)

Under **s.9(6)**, registration may be **revoked or suspended** where the agent:
- is in **breach of the conditions** of registration;
- makes a **wilful default** or contravenes the Act, rules or regulations;
- is found to have obtained registration through **misrepresentation or fraud**.

An **opportunity of being heard** must be given.

### 2.9 Penalties ⚖️

| Provision | Trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **s.62** | Contravention of **s.9 or s.10** | **₹10,000 per day** of continuing default, cumulatively up to **5% of the cost** of the plot/apartment/building |
| **s.65** | Failure to comply with an **Authority** order | Daily penalty, cumulatively up to **5%** of the estimated cost |
| **s.66** | Failure to comply with an **Appellate Tribunal** order | **Imprisonment up to 1 year** and/or fine up to **10%** of the estimated cost |
| **s.69** | Offences by companies | Persons in charge and responsible are deemed guilty |
| **Order 46C/2025** | Advertising non-compliance | **₹10,000–₹50,000 per violation**; 10-day cure or continuing offence |

Put a number on it: on a **₹2 crore** flat, the s.62 ceiling is **₹10 lakh**. Very few brokerage
cheques survive that arithmetic.

### 2.10 Step-by-step on the portal (syllabus 5.8)

**5.8.1 Individual agents:** account → personal details (PAN, Aadhaar, photo) → business address →
ITRs (3 years) → litigation declaration → Certificate of Competency → declaration → **₹10,000 + GST** → application number → certificate.

**5.8.2 Non-individual agents:** account in the entity's name → entity details and incorporation
documents → **MOA/AOA, partnership deed or LLP agreement** → registered office proof → details of
**all partners/directors** → entity ITRs → litigation declaration for entity and officers →
authorised signatory + their Certificate of Competency → declaration → **₹1,00,000 + GST** → application number → certificate.

> 💼 The most common rejection reasons are mismatched names across PAN/Aadhaar/bank, illegible
> uploads, and missing ITRs. Fix those before you submit.

## 3. 🔴 Agent Compliance Checklist

Print this. It is the operational heart of the module.

### ☐ Before advertising
- [ ] My **agent registration** is valid and unexpired
- [ ] The **project is MahaRERA-registered** and status is active
- [ ] Ad carries the **MahaRERA project registration number**
- [ ] Ad carries **maharera.maharashtra.gov.in**
- [ ] Ad carries a **scannable QR code**, correct aspect ratio
- [ ] Registration number/website font **≥ largest font used for my contact details** ⚖️ Order 46C/2025
- [ ] Number, website and QR in the **top-right corner**
- [ ] My **own agent registration number** is displayed
- [ ] Every claim in the ad is traceable to the **portal disclosure**
- [ ] No amenity, approval or possession date claimed that isn't disclosed

### ☐ Before showing a property
- [ ] Ran the **15-step investigation** (Module 4)
- [ ] Read the **proforma agreement** and any **deviation sheet**
- [ ] Know the **RERA carpet area** and can show where it's stated
- [ ] Know the **CC coverage** and the **completion date**
- [ ] Checked **litigation** and **complaints** against the promoter
- [ ] Can name the **legal promoter entity**, not just the brand

### ☐ Before accepting a booking
- [ ] Buyer has seen the **MahaRERA disclosure** — I sent the link
- [ ] Buyer has the **cost sheet** with carpet area, all charges, taxes broken out
- [ ] Buyer has the **draft allotment letter** and **draft agreement**
- [ ] Booking amount is within **10%** of the cost, or a registered AFS is being executed ⚖️ s.13
- [ ] Payments go to the promoter's **collection account** — never to me personally
- [ ] Nothing promised orally that isn't in writing

### ☐ Before taking brokerage
- [ ] There is a written **brokerage agreement** — scope, rate, trigger event
- [ ] I have raised a proper **invoice** (GST if registered)
- [ ] I understand **TDS u/s 194H** will be deducted (⏱ 2% — see Module 10)
- [ ] The transaction is recorded in my **books** ⚖️ s.10(b)
- [ ] I am not taking fees from **both sides** without disclosing it to both

### ☐ Before recommending a project
- [ ] I would put my **own family** in it
- [ ] Risks are disclosed to the client **in writing**
- [ ] I have not overstated **appreciation** or **rental yield**
- [ ] I've explained **what happens if it's delayed** (s.18)
- [ ] I'm not recommending it only because the brokerage is higher

### ☐ During the transaction
- [ ] Buyer has the **registered** Agreement for Sale, not just a signed one
- [ ] Payment schedule is **construction-linked** and matches the agreement
- [ ] Every promise from the developer is captured in the agreement
- [ ] I am keeping copies of everything

### ☐ After the transaction
- [ ] Complete file **retained** ⚖️ s.10(b)
- [ ] Buyer knows to take possession **within 2 months of OC** ⚖️ s.19(10)
- [ ] Buyer knows about the **5-year defect liability** ⚖️ s.14(3)
- [ ] Buyer knows how to complain to MahaRERA if something goes wrong
- [ ] My **renewal date** is in the calendar

## 8. Case study 📋

**The friendly referral.**

A former client asks you to help their cousin buy a resale flat in a 2012 building — completed
long before RERA, never registered. You're not advertising, not representing the developer; you
just introduce buyer to seller and take **₹2 lakh**.

Separately, the same month, you introduce a buyer to a **pre-launch** in an unregistered
under-construction project and take ₹3 lakh.

**Question: are you in breach in either case? Both? Neither?**

<details><summary>Analysis</summary>

**Case 1 — the 2012 resale: not a s.10(a) breach.** s.10(a) prohibits facilitating a sale in a
project that **is required to be registered** but isn't. A building completed before the Act
commenced, with its completion certificate obtained pre-commencement, falls outside the
registration requirement. There is no unregistered *project* being marketed here.

But note carefully: **s.9 still describes you as a real estate agent**, and you are taking a fee
for negotiating a transfer. The prudent position — and the one MahaRERA's own guidance points to —
is that **an agent operating in Maharashtra registers and stays registered**, and confines the
question to *which projects* they may deal in. Doing resale work without registration while
holding yourself out as an agent is not a position worth defending.

**Case 2 — the pre-launch: a clear breach of s.10(a).** The project requires registration and
doesn't have it. Penalty under **s.62**: ₹10,000 per day of continuing default, up to **5% of the
cost** of the apartment. On a ₹2.5 crore flat that's a ceiling of ₹12.5 lakh against your ₹3 lakh fee.
Your registration is also exposed under **s.9(6)**.

**The distinction to carry away:** RERA regulates **projects requiring registration**. Your
personal registration is the baseline; the project's registration determines whether you may deal
in it at all. Check both, every time — and when in doubt about an old building, check whether a
completion certificate pre-dates the Act.
</details>

## 9. Quiz — 10 questions

1. State the two limbs of the s.2(zm) definition.
2. Registration fee for an individual and for a company in Maharashtra? Validity?
3. Is a sole proprietorship an individual or non-individual for fees?
4. List four things s.10 prohibits.
5. What is the s.62 penalty — daily rate and ceiling?
6. On what three grounds may registration be revoked under s.9(6)?
7. What is the exam pattern and passing mark per the certification guidelines?
8. Name three documents required for individual registration.
9. What happens if you keep dealing after your registration expires?
10. Under Order 46C/2025, where must the QR code appear and how large must the registration number be?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. (i) Negotiates or acts on behalf of another in a transfer of a plot/apartment/building in a real estate project, **and** (ii) receives remuneration, fees or any other charge.
2. **₹10,000 + GST** individual; **₹1,00,000 + GST** non-individual; valid **5 years** unless revoked. ⏱ verify.
3. Treated as **individual**.
4. Facilitating sale in an unregistered project; unfair trade practice/misrepresentation; false or misleading representation of services; permitting publication of an advertisement of services not intended to be offered. (Also: failing to maintain books; failing to facilitate documents to the allottee.)
5. **₹10,000 per day** of continuing default, cumulatively up to **5% of the cost** of the plot/apartment/building.
6. Breach of conditions of registration; wilful default or contravention of the Act/rules/regulations; registration obtained by misrepresentation or fraud. An opportunity of being heard must be given.
7. **50 MCQs × 2 marks = 100**; pass at **40**; fee capped at **₹1,500** excl. GST; Marathi or English. ⏱ verify current pattern.
8. Any three of: PAN, Aadhaar/address proof, photograph, business address proof, **3 years' ITRs**, litigation declaration, letterhead/stamp, **Certificate of Competency**.
9. You are dealing **without registration** — s.9 breach, s.62 penalty at ₹10,000/day.
10. **Top-right corner**; registration number and website in a font **equal to or larger than the largest font used for contact details/address**; QR scannable with correct aspect ratio. Penalty ₹10,000–₹50,000 per violation.
</details>

## 12. One-page revision sheet

> **You are an agent if:** you negotiate/act for another in a transfer **and** take a fee. By whatever name called.
>
> **s.9** register · **s.10** conduct · **s.62** penalty (**₹10,000/day, 5% ceiling**) ·
> **s.65** disobey Authority · **s.66** disobey Tribunal (**1 yr jail / 10%**) · **s.69** companies.
>
> **⏱ Fees:** ₹10,000 + GST individual (incl. sole proprietor) · ₹1,00,000 + GST non-individual.
> **Validity 5 years.** Renew via the **Renewal tab**, before expiry. Reminder at 4y 9m.
>
> **Exam:** 50 MCQ × 2 = 100 · pass **40** · fee ≤ **₹1,500** + GST · Marathi or English.
>
> **s.10 forbids:** unregistered projects · unfair trade practice · misrepresentation · false ads.
> **s.10 requires:** keep books · give the allottee their documents.
>
> **Revocation (s.9(6)):** breach of conditions · wilful default/contravention · fraud. Hearing required.
>
> **Every ad:** project reg. no. + website + QR, **top-right**, font ≥ your contact details, plus your own agent number.
>
> **Never:** market before registration · take money in your own account · promise what isn't disclosed.
