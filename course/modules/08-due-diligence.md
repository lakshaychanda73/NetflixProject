## 1. What I need to learn

How to find the problem **before** your client's money moves.

Everything in this module is one idea: **every claim must trace to a document, and every document
must trace to an issuing authority.** A statement with no document behind it is marketing.

> **Scope note:** the official syllabus for this module doesn't separately list **Occupancy
> Certificate** or **encumbrance search**. Both are included below because no competent agent works
> without them — but they are marked **📌 beyond-syllabus** so you know what's examinable and what's
> professional practice.

## 2. 🔴 Professional Real-Estate Due-Diligence Checklist

### A. Promoter and registration

| Check | Document / source | Where to verify | 🚩 Red flag | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Promoter legal identity | Registration record; incorporation docs | [MahaRERA project search](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/) | Brand ≠ legal entity; brand-new SPV with no history | Get the exact entity name; search its other projects |
| Promoter track record | s.4(2)(b) disclosure — last 5 years | MahaRERA promoter search | Delays, lapsed registrations, repeated extensions | Score under the Module 6 Framework |
| Project registration | Registration number & status | MahaRERA project record | Not registered / **lapsed** / de-registered | **Stop.** s.10(a) applies to you |
| Registration validity | Proposed completion date; extensions | Project record | Date passed; multiple extensions | Ask for the original date and each extension's ground |
| Phase coverage | Phase-wise registrations | Project record | Tower being sold isn't in any registration | **Stop.** Each phase registers separately |
| Complaints & orders | Decided matters | [Orders & Judgments](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/orders-judgements) | Multiple s.18 complaints; non-compliance with orders | Disclose in writing to the client |

### B. Title and land

| Check | Document / source | Where to verify | 🚩 Red flag | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Title of property | **Title Certificate / Search Report** by an advocate (30-year search customary 💼) | Promoter; independent advocate | No title certificate; search shorter than 30 years; qualified opinion | Commission an **independent** search — never rely only on the promoter's |
| Land ownership | 7/12 extract (rural) or **Property Card** (urban); Index II | [IGR Maharashtra](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/); City Survey Office | Owner ≠ promoter with no explanation | Trace the chain document by document |
| Development rights | **Development Agreement / JDA**, Power of Attorney, registered | IGR (registered document) | Unregistered DA; expired POA; landowner not a co-promoter | Insist the landowner be a co-promoter on the registration |
| Chain of title | Prior sale deeds, mutation entries | IGR; revenue records | Gap in the chain; unexplained transfer | Do not proceed until reconciled |
| Land use / zoning | Development Plan remarks; **DCPR 2034** | MCGM / planning authority | Land reserved for a public purpose; wrong zone | Get DP remarks in writing |
| 📌 **Encumbrances** | **Encumbrance/search report**; charge registers | IGR; MCA (for company charges) | Undisclosed mortgage; lender charge with no NOC route | Ask which lender, and the per-unit NOC process |

### C. Approvals and construction

| Check | Document / source | Where to verify | 🚩 Red flag | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IOD** | Intimation of Disapproval with conditions | MCGM / planning authority; [AutoDCR](https://autodcr.mcgm.gov.in/bpamsclient2/Login.aspx) | Conditions unmet | Read the conditions, not just the cover page |
| **Commencement Certificate** | CC with **floor coverage stated** | MahaRERA record; AutoDCR | No CC; CC covers fewer floors than sold | **Match CC floors against the unit's floor.** Mismatch = stop |
| Sanctioned plans | Approved floor & layout plans | MahaRERA uploads; AutoDCR | Brochure shows towers/amenities absent from the plan | Compare page by page |
| NOCs | Fire, environment, aviation, traffic, water | Promoter; issuing authority | Missing on a high-rise | Ask for each, with date and authority |
| Construction progress | **QPR**, Forms 1/2/3 | MahaRERA project updates | Missing quarters; flat percentages | Cross-check with a site visit |
| 📌 **Occupancy Certificate** | **OC** (full or part), wing/floor coverage | MCGM; [Check application status](https://portal.mcgm.gov.in/) | Possession offered with no OC; part-OC not covering the unit | **Never allow possession without an OC covering that unit** |

### D. Financial and statutory

| Check | Document / source | Where to verify | 🚩 Red flag | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Status of tax payments** | Property tax receipts; municipal dues | MCGM property tax portal; promoter | Arrears; dues carried into the society | Get current receipts; make clearance a condition |
| Project bank accounts | Collection / Separate / Transaction | Registration disclosure | Accounts encumbered, or not with one scheduled bank | Ask which bank; confirm the 70% account exists |
| Ready reckoner value | **Annual Statement of Rates** | [IGR e-ASR](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/Home/asr_about) | Agreement value far below ASR | Stamp duty is charged on the **higher** of the two — see Module 10 |
| GST position | Cost sheet; OC status | Promoter; cost sheet | GST charged on a **completed** (OC-received) unit | ⏱ Under-construction only — see Module 10 |
| Loan eligibility | Lender's **APF** / project approval list | Bank/HFC | No major lender has approved the project | A lender's refusal is a due-diligence signal, free of charge |

### E. Documents and area

| Check | Document / source | Where to verify | 🚩 Red flag | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Allotment letter** | Proforma uploaded under s.4(2)(g) | MahaRERA record | Not uploaded; cancellation deduction above the capped 2% | Compare against MahaRERA's model allotment letter |
| **Agreement for Sale** | Proforma + **deviation sheet** | MahaRERA record | Deviations from Annexure A not highlighted | Read the deviation sheet line by line |
| **MahaRERA carpet area** | Declared carpet area for the unit type | MahaRERA apartment details | Cost sheet quotes "saleable"/"super built-up"; agreement carpet < disclosed carpet | Get carpet **in writing**, and matched across all three documents |
| Parking | Parking disclosure; sanctioned plan | MahaRERA; plans | Parking "sold" separately with no basis in the plan | Confirm what is actually allotted and how |
| Common areas & amenities | Disclosure; sanctioned plan | MahaRERA | Brochure amenities absent from the plan | Only what's in the plan and the agreement is real |
| Payment schedule | Agreement's payment plan | Draft agreement | Front-loaded, not construction-linked; >10% before registered AFS | ⚖️ s.13 — flag immediately |

## 3. The three cross-checks that catch most problems

Most defects surface from three comparisons. Do these every single time.

**① Brochure vs sanctioned plan.** Towers, floors, unit mix, amenities. Anything in the brochure
and not in the plan does not exist yet.

**② CC floor coverage vs the unit's floor.** The single most under-checked item in Mumbai
new-build sales.

**③ Carpet area — disclosure vs cost sheet vs draft agreement.** All three must state the **same
number**. If they don't, you've found either an error or a practice.

## 4. 📋 Case study — walking a project through

> **Honesty note.** I could not access the MahaRERA portal from the environment this course was
> built in, so I have **not** reproduced any real project's registration number, QPR history or
> disclosures — inventing those would be worse than useless to you. Instead:
> **Part A** is a fully worked **constructed composite** (clearly labelled) built from the real
> patterns described in this module, so the method hangs together end to end.
> **Part B** is the live assignment on a real project, which is the version that actually trains you.

### Part A — worked example *(constructed composite — not a real project)*

**"Sunteck-style redevelopment, Borivali East"** — 2 towers, 34 floors, 3BHK, quoted ₹2.95 crore.

| Step | Finding | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Search | Found under a project name different from the brand on the hoarding | Normal — SPV. Note the legal entity |
| 2 Status | Registered, active; completion Dec 2027 | ✅ |
| 3 Promoter | SPV incorporated 2021; parent has 6 completed projects, 2 delayed by ~14 months | 🟠 Track record mixed |
| 4 Progress | Declared 38%; 3 slabs cast | ✅ Consistent |
| 5 Completion date | Original Dec 2026 → extended once to Dec 2027, ground: approval delay | 🟠 Approval delay is arguably **not** force majeure. Ask |
| 6 Land | Society redevelopment; society owns land, developer holds development rights | 🚩 **Society is not a co-promoter** |
| 7 Approvals | IOD present; **CC covers up to floor 22** | 🚩 Unit is on **floor 29** |
| 8 Plans | Sanctioned plan shows 2 towers ✅; brochure shows a rooftop infinity pool **not in the plan** | 🚩 |
| 9 Buildings | Unit exists in disclosed inventory | ✅ |
| 10 Carpet | Disclosure **968 sq ft**; cost sheet quotes "1,340 sq ft saleable" | 🟠 Loading 38% — must be stated as carpet |
| 11 QPR | 4 consecutive filings, on time | ✅ Good signal |
| 12 Litigation | One pending society dispute over the redevelopment agreement | 🚩 **Title-adjacent** |
| 13 Encumbrance | Construction finance mortgage disclosed, NOC per unit at registration | ✅ Normal and disclosed |
| 14 Possession | Not applicable yet | — |
| 15 Other | Proformas uploaded; **deviation sheet present but deviations not highlighted** | 🚩 |

**The five-line note:**
> 34-floor society redevelopment in Borivali East by an SPV of a mid-tier developer with a mixed
> delivery record. QPR discipline is good and progress is real. **Three live issues: the CC covers
> 22 floors against a 29th-floor unit; the society is not a co-promoter despite owning the land;
> and there is a pending society dispute on the redevelopment agreement.** The 38% loading means
> the real price is ₹30,475/carpet sq ft, not the ₹22,015/sq ft being quoted. **Not recommendable
> until the amended CC and the society dispute status are produced in writing.**

Notice what happened: nothing here required insider access. Every finding came from the portal, the
brochure and arithmetic. That is the whole job.

### Part B — your live assignment 🔴

Pick a **real** under-construction project in Mumbai, Thane or Navi Mumbai — one you can see
advertised. Then:

1. Run the **15-step investigation** (Module 4) and the **checklist** above.
2. Pull the actual registration number, promoter entity, completion date, QPR history and carpet
   area **from the live portal**.
3. Cross-check land ownership at **[IGR Maharashtra](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/)** and
   building approvals at **[MCGM AutoDCR](https://autodcr.mcgm.gov.in/bpamsclient2/Login.aspx)**.
4. Search the promoter at **[Orders & Judgments](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/orders-judgements)**.
5. Run the three cross-checks in §3.
6. Write your own five-line note.

Do this for **three projects**. After the third, you will be better at this than most working agents.

## 9. Quiz — 10 questions

1. Name the seven due-diligence heads in the official syllabus for this module.
2. Where do you verify land ownership in urban Mumbai, and what document do you look for?
3. What is the customary search period for a title report? Is that law or practice?
4. What must you match the CC against?
5. Where do you check whether a project's promoter has adverse MahaRERA orders?
6. Which three documents must state the same carpet area?
7. What does a part-OC mean for a buyer's possession?
8. Stamp duty is calculated on which value?
9. Why is a lender's refusal to approve a project useful information?
10. What is the cap on deduction if an allottee cancels, under the model allotment letter?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Verify promoter identity & project details on MahaRERA · title of property · building approvals & commencement certificate · status of tax payments · litigations against the project · allotment letter & model form of agreement · MahaRERA carpet area.
2. **IGR Maharashtra** / City Survey Office — the **Property Card** (urban) or 7/12 extract (rural), plus Index II for registered documents.
3. **30 years** — that is **💼 practice/custom**, not a statutory requirement. Don't present it as law.
4. The **floor of the unit being sold**. A CC covering fewer floors than the unit is a stop.
5. [MahaRERA Orders & Judgments](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/orders-judgements), searching by promoter as well as project.
6. The **MahaRERA disclosure**, the **cost sheet**, and the **draft Agreement for Sale**.
7. It covers only specified wings/floors. If the buyer's unit isn't covered, there is effectively **no OC for that unit** and possession should not be taken.
8. The **higher** of the agreement value and the **ready reckoner (ASR)** value.
9. Lenders run their own independent legal and technical due diligence before granting project approval (APF). A refusal is a free second opinion.
10. **2%** — MahaRERA's model allotment letter caps the deduction on cancellation by the allottee at 2%.
</details>

## 12. One-page revision sheet

> **Rule:** every claim traces to a **document**; every document traces to an **issuing authority**.
>
> **Five heads:** Promoter/registration · Title & land · Approvals & construction · Financial & statutory · Documents & area.
>
> **The three cross-checks:**
> ① brochure vs **sanctioned plan** ② **CC floor coverage** vs the unit's floor
> ③ **carpet area**: disclosure = cost sheet = agreement
>
> **The five stops:** no/lapsed registration · unit not in registered inventory · CC doesn't cover
> the floor · possession without a covering OC · undisclosed title litigation or encumbrance.
>
> **Where to look:** MahaRERA (registration, disclosures, QPR, orders) · **IGR** (title, Index II,
> encumbrance, ASR) · **MCGM AutoDCR** (IOD, CC, plans, OC) · **MCA** (company charges) · lender's APF.
>
> **Stamp duty on the HIGHER of agreement value and ASR.**
> **Cancellation deduction capped at 2%** (model allotment letter).
> **30-year title search = 💼 practice, not law.**
