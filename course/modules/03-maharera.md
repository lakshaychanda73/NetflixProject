## 2. Simple explanation

### 2.1 RERA vs MahaRERA — get this right permanently

| | **RERA** | **MahaRERA** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A **central Act of Parliament** — Act 16 of 2016 | The **regulatory authority for Maharashtra**, constituted under that Act |
| Made by | Parliament of India | Government of Maharashtra (s.20) |
| Applies | All India | Maharashtra only |
| Changes how | Amendment by Parliament | Orders, circulars, regulations by the Authority; Rules by the State Government |
| You'd cite it as | "Section 13 of the Act" | "MahaRERA Order No. 46C/2025" |

**Say it once, correctly:** *RERA is the law. MahaRERA is the regulator that enforces the law in
Maharashtra.* There is no such thing as "the MahaRERA Act". If you write that in the exam, you
lose the mark.

The three-layer stack you are actually governed by:

```
LAYER 1  RERA Act, 2016 (Parliament)              ── the principles
LAYER 2  Maharashtra RERA Rules, 2017 (State Govt) ── the numbers: fees, interest, forms
LAYER 3  MahaRERA Regulations / Orders / Circulars ── the current operating rules
```

Layer 3 changes several times a year. **This is the layer where agents get caught out**, because
they learned the rules once in 2019 and never re-read.

### 2.2 Key pillars of MahaRERA (syllabus 3.1)

**Pillar 1 — Registration.** Projects (s.3–5) and agents (s.9). Everything begins here: nothing
gets a legal existence in Maharashtra real estate marketing until it has a MahaRERA number.

**Pillar 2 — Disclosure.** A public, searchable web database (s.34(b)). Project details,
promoter details, approvals, sanctioned plans, quarterly progress, litigation. This is the pillar
you'll use every working day — Module 4 is entirely about it.

**Pillar 3 — Compliance monitoring.** Quarterly Progress Reports, the separate-account regime,
Forms 1/2/3, advertising norms, agent certification. MahaRERA has moved steadily from a
registration body to a **monitoring** body.

**Pillar 4 — Dispute resolution.** Three routes with different characters:
**Conciliation** (voluntary, fast, no adjudication) → **Complaint to the Authority** (s.31)
→ **Adjudicating Officer** (s.71, compensation only) → **Appeal to the Appellate Tribunal**
(s.43, 60 days) → High Court (s.58, question of law).

**Pillar 5 — Enforcement.** Orders, penalties, recovery as **arrears of land revenue** (s.40),
project **de-registration**, and suspension/revocation of agent registration.

### 2.3 Powers and functions of the Authority

Drawn from ss.34–38:

- **s.34** — register and regulate projects and agents; maintain the website database; ensure compliance.
- **s.35** — call for information and conduct investigations; **powers of a civil court** in respect of summoning, discovery, evidence.
- **s.36** — issue **interim orders** during an inquiry.
- **s.37** — issue **directions** binding on promoters, agents and allottees.
- **s.38** — impose penalty or interest; regulate its own procedure.
- **s.40** — **recover** dues as arrears of land revenue.

The practical meaning for you: MahaRERA can summon you, can order you, and can recover money from
you through the revenue machinery. It is not a grievance desk.

### 2.4 How MahaRERA regulates *agents* specifically

Four levers, all of which are examinable:

1. **Registration** (s.9 + Maharashtra Rules) — mandatory, 5-year validity.
2. **Competency certification** — MahaRERA made training + a certification exam a condition of new
   registration and renewal. This is the reason your MATP course exists.
3. **Advertising discipline** — the registration number, website address and **QR code** must appear
   in agents' advertisements to prescribed standards.
4. **Penalty and revocation** — s.62, s.65, s.66, plus suspension/revocation of registration.

### 2.5 How MahaRERA regulates *promoters*

Registration and disclosure under ss.3–5; **QPR** filing on a financial-quarter cycle; the
**three-bank-account** structure; model **allotment letter** and **agreement for sale** proformas;
advertising norms; and de-registration of stalled projects.

## 7. Practical exercise ✍️

**The regulatory currency audit (45 minutes).**

1. Open https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/order and https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/circular
2. List **every order and circular issued in the last 12 months**.
3. For each, write one line: *does this affect promoters, agents, or allottees?*
4. Star the ones that affect **agents**.
5. Put a recurring reminder in your calendar to repeat this **every quarter**.

This is not busywork — it is the single habit that separates a compliant agent from one who is
operating on 2019 rules.

## 8. Case study 📋

**The stale advertisement.**

You've been running a hoarding and an Instagram campaign for a Thane project for eight months.
The creative shows the project name, your phone number in large type, and the MahaRERA project
registration number in small text at the bottom. There is no QR code and your own agent
registration number does not appear.

**Questions:**
1. Which order governs this, and what does it require?
2. What is the exposure per violation?
3. Is "the developer designed the creative" a defence?
4. What do you change, today?

<details><summary>Analysis</summary>

1. **Order No. 46C/2025 (8 April 2025)**. It requires that the MahaRERA registration number and
   the website address be displayed in a font **equal to or larger than the largest font used for
   contact details/address**, that the registration number, website address and **QR code** be
   placed in the **top-right corner**, and that the QR code be scannable with correct aspect ratio.
2. **₹10,000 to ₹50,000 per violation**, and failure to correct within **10 days** is treated as a
   **continuing offence** with further action under the Act.
3. **No.** The order binds *promoters and registered real estate agents*. If the advertisement is
   yours — your number, your leads — it is your compliance obligation. Practically: never run a
   developer's creative without checking it against the order yourself.
4. Rebuild the creative: QR + registration number + website in the top-right, font sized against
   your own contact details, your agent registration number displayed, and re-check every live
   asset — hoardings, portals, social, WhatsApp forwards, brochures.

**The point:** your phone number being the biggest thing on the ad is exactly what the order
targets. Advertising compliance is the most common and most easily avoided agent penalty.
</details>

## 9. Quiz — 10 questions

1. Is MahaRERA created by a Maharashtra Act? Explain.
2. Name the three layers of the regulatory stack and who makes each.
3. Which section gives the Authority civil-court powers of summoning and discovery?
4. Under which section are MahaRERA dues recovered, and by what mechanism?
5. Name the five pillars of MahaRERA.
6. What does Order 46C/2025 require, and what is the penalty range?
7. Name the three bank accounts a promoter must now maintain.
8. Which document is effectively the blueprint for your certification exam?
9. What is the appeal route above the Authority, and the time limit?
10. Where would you check whether a MahaRERA rule you learned two years ago is still current?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. **No.** MahaRERA is constituted by the **Government of Maharashtra under s.20 of the central RERA Act, 2016**. There is no separate "MahaRERA Act".
2. Layer 1 **RERA Act 2016** (Parliament) · Layer 2 **Maharashtra RERA Rules 2017** (State Government) · Layer 3 **Regulations/Orders/Circulars** (MahaRERA itself).
3. **Section 35.**
4. **Section 40** — recoverable as **arrears of land revenue**.
5. Registration · Disclosure · Compliance monitoring · Dispute resolution · Enforcement.
6. QR code, MahaRERA registration number and website address in the **top-right** of all advertisements, registration number/website in a font **≥ the largest font used for contact details**, QR scannable. Penalty **₹10,000–₹50,000 per violation**; uncorrected after **10 days** = continuing offence.
7. **Collection** account (all receipts) · **Separate** account (70%) · **Transaction** account (up to 30%).
8. The **Guidelines for Real Estate Agent Training and Certification** (`120.pdf`).
9. **Real Estate Appellate Tribunal**, within **60 days** (s.43(5)); promoter pre-deposits ≥30%.
10. The live **[/order](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/order)** and **[/circular](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/circular)** indexes on the MahaRERA site.
</details>
