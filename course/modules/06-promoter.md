## 2. Simple explanation

### 2.1 Registration of real estate projects (syllabus 6.1) ⚖️ s.3

No promoter shall **advertise, market, book, sell or offer for sale, or invite persons to
purchase** any plot, apartment or building in any planning area **without registering the project**.

**Exemptions — s.3(2):**

| Exemption | Detail |
|---|---|
| **(a) Small projects** | Land **not exceeding 500 sq m** *or* apartments **not exceeding 8**, inclusive of all phases. State Government may reduce these thresholds. |
| **(b) Completed projects** | Completion certificate received **before commencement of the Act**. |
| **(c) Renovation/repair/redevelopment** | Where it does **not involve marketing, advertising, selling or new allotment**. |

**Two things people get wrong:**
- The threshold is **"or"**, not "and" — a 400 sq m plot with 12 apartments still needs registration.
- **Each phase is a separate project** and registers separately. "Phase 2 is covered by Phase 1's number" is wrong.

### 2.2 Registration application & process (syllabus 6.2) ⚖️ s.4

The promoter applies to the Authority with prescribed fee and documents. **s.4(2)** requires:

| Clause | Disclosure |
|---|---|
| (a) | Promoter details — name, address, type of enterprise, registered particulars |
| (b) | **Track record** — projects launched in the last 5 years, status, delays, pending cases |
| (c) | Authenticated copy of **approvals and commencement certificate** |
| (d) | **Sanctioned plan, layout plan, specifications** |
| (e) | Plan of development works, proposed facilities, fire-fighting, drinking water, emergency management |
| (f) | **Location details** with clear demarcation of land |
| (g) | **Proforma of the allotment letter, agreement for sale and conveyance deed** |
| (h) | Number, type and **carpet area** of apartments; number and areas of garages |
| (i) | Names and addresses of **real estate agents**, contractors, architect, structural engineer |
| (j) | Declaration of legal title with authenticated documents |
| (k) | Details of **encumbrances**, including litigation, and any rights/title/interest/name of a party in possession |
| (l) | **Declaration supported by affidavit** — including the **70% separate account**, timelines, and no false statement |

**s.4(2)(l)(D) — the 70% rule.** Seventy per cent of amounts realised from allottees goes into a
**separate account in a scheduled bank**, used only to cover **land cost and construction cost**,
withdrawn in proportion to completion, certified by **Architect, Engineer and CA**.

**Grant of registration — s.5.** The Authority grants or rejects within **30 days**. If it neither
grants nor rejects, the project is **deemed registered** and the Authority provides the
registration number. Rejection requires an opportunity of being heard.

### 2.3 ⏱ The three-bank-account structure (current Maharashtra practice)

MahaRERA operationalised the 70% rule into a **three-account structure**
(Order No. MahaRERA/Secy/129/2024 — [PDF](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/Orders_and_circulars/BANK_ACCOUNT_ORDER.pdf)):

| Account | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Collection account** | Receives **all** collections from allottees — including parking, amenities and other charges — excluding taxes and statutory dues |
| **Separate account** | **70%** of collections transferred here; land + construction cost only |
| **Transaction account** | Up to **30%** transferred here for the promoter's other project costs |

All three must be with a **single scheduled bank**, opened **before applying** for registration.
They must be **free of encumbrance** — not escrow, no lien, no loan, no third-party (lender) control.

**Why an agent cares:** you can tell a buyer exactly where their money goes and why it's protected.
That is a genuinely reassuring, genuinely accurate thing to be able to say — and it's a good test
of a promoter: ask which bank the three accounts are with.

### 2.4 Extension of registration (syllabus 6.3) ⚖️ s.6

- Registration may be extended on application, **ordinarily up to one year**.
- The ground is **force majeure** — war, flood, drought, fire, cyclone, earthquake or other calamity affecting development.
- The Authority may, in reasonable circumstances and without default by the promoter, extend for reasons recorded in writing.
- Not available where the promoter is simply late.

🚩 **Agent reading:** one extension with a stated cause is normal. **Three extensions is a pattern**,
and a pattern is a forecast.

### 2.5 Revocation of registration (syllabus 6.4) ⚖️ s.7

The Authority may revoke on complaint or suo motu where the promoter:
- **(a)** makes default in doing anything required under the Act, rules or regulations;
- **(b)** violates any of the terms or conditions of the approval given by the competent authority;
- **(c)** is involved in any kind of **unfair practice or irregularities** — including false/misleading advertisements, and any fraudulent practice.

Minimum **30 days' notice** in writing and an opportunity to be heard. Instead of revoking, the
Authority may permit registration to continue subject to further terms.

**Consequences — s.8.** On lapse or revocation the Authority:
- bars the promoter from accessing the website and lists them as a **defaulter**;
- informs other state authorities;
- may, after consulting the appropriate government, **allow the association of allottees to complete the project**, or take such other action as it deems fit.

### 2.6 Functions and duties of the promoter (syllabus 6.5) ⚖️ ss.11–18

| Section | Duty | What you check |
|---|---|---|
| **11(1)** | Create a **web page on the Authority's website** and keep it updated | Is the record current? |
| **11(1)(b)** | **Quarterly updates** on project status | Four filings, on time |
| **11(3)(a)** | At time of booking, give the allottee **sanctioned plans, layout, specifications** | Did the buyer actually get these? |
| **11(3)(b)** | Give **stage-wise time schedule** of completion | Is it in writing? |
| **11(4)(a)** | Responsible for **all obligations until conveyance** to the allottee/association | Who's on the hook, and until when |
| **11(4)(e)** | **Form the association** of allottees | Post-possession |
| **11(4)(f)** | Execute **registered conveyance** | Deemed conveyance disputes |
| **11(4)(g)** | Pay all **outgoings** until transfer | Unpaid dues become the society's problem |
| **11(5)** | **No advertisement** without the registration number and website | Order 46C/2025 sits on top of this |
| **12** | **Veracity of advertisement/prospectus** — allottee who relied and paid may **withdraw with full refund + interest**, plus compensation | Every claim in your marketing |
| **13** | **No more than 10%** without a **registered** Agreement for Sale | The most-broken rule in the market |
| **14** | **Adhere to sanctioned plans**; minor additions allowed; other alterations need **2/3rds allottee consent**; **5-year defect liability**, rectify in **30 days** | Brochure vs sanctioned plan |
| **15** | **No transfer of the project** to a third party without **2/3rds allottees** + Authority approval | Change of developer mid-project |
| **16** | **Insurance** of title and construction | Rarely checked; worth asking |
| **17** | **Transfer of title** — conveyance to the association within the specified period | Society formation |
| **18** | **Return of amount and compensation** — failure to complete/give possession → **refund with interest** + compensation, or **interest for every month of delay** if the allottee chooses to stay | Your buyer's core protection |

### 2.7 Quarterly Progress Reports ⏱

Maharashtra runs QPR on a **financial-quarter** basis: filed within **20 days of quarter end** —
due by the **20th of July, October, January and April**.

The certificate forms:

| Form | Signed by | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 1** | **Architect** | Certifies percentage completion of construction — for withdrawal from the separate account |
| **Form 2** | **Structural Engineer** | Certifies cost incurred on construction |
| **Form 2A** | — | **Quality Assurance Certificate** — within three months after each financial year end |
| **Form 3** | **Chartered Accountant** | Certifies cost incurred on land and construction; withdrawal proportionality |
| **Form 4** | **Architect** | Certificate on **completion** of the project |
| **Form 5** | **CA** | **Annual Statement of Accounts** — within six months after financial year end |

Forms 1, 2 and 3 need not be uploaded every quarter, but must be **maintained by the promoter** and
submitted to the bank to release money from the separate account. Missing a QPR deadline triggers a
**web-generated notice** with 10 days to comply, and **non-compliance is displayed publicly** on the portal.

## 3. 🔴 Developer Project Analysis Framework

How an agent decides whether to put a client into a project. Score each dimension 1–5; anything
scoring 1 on **A, B or F** is a stop, not a discount.

### A. Legal standing *(gate — pass/fail)*
- Registration **active**, not lapsed or de-registered
- **CC** covers the floors being sold
- Land title clean; if JDA, **landowner is a co-promoter**
- No title litigation
- Proforma agreement uploaded; deviations highlighted
> **Fail any of these → do not proceed.** No price makes this back.

### B. Promoter strength
- Completed projects in the last 5 years, and **did they finish on time?**
- Other registrations: any lapsed, de-registered, repeatedly extended?
- Complaints and adverse orders at [Orders & Judgments](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/orders-judgements)
- Is the promoter an SPV? Who stands behind it?
- Financial signals: is construction finance in place, or is the project funded purely from sales?

### C. Delivery credibility
- Declared completion date vs actual progress
- **Four consecutive QPRs**, on time, showing movement
- Extensions taken and grounds given
- Site visit: does the physical work match the declared percentage?

### D. Product quality
- Carpet area **efficiency** (carpet ÷ saleable — see Module 10)
- Unit layout, floor plate, ventilation, parking ratio
- Amenities in the **sanctioned plan**, not just the brochure
- Specifications in the **agreement**, not just the show flat

### E. Commercial terms
- Price per **carpet** sq ft vs comparable projects in the micro-market
- All-in cost: GST, stamp duty, registration, floor rise, parking, club, maintenance advance
- Payment plan: is it genuinely **construction-linked**?
- Cancellation terms — the model allotment letter caps deduction at **2%** (Module 9)

### F. Client fit *(gate)*
- End-user or investor? Under-construction suits neither automatically
- Can they carry EMI + rent through a delay?
- Is the exit horizon realistic for this micro-market?
> **If the product doesn't fit the client, a good project is still the wrong recommendation.**

**Then write the five-line note from Module 4.** If you can't write it without hedging, you don't understand the project well enough to sell it.

## 7. Practical exercise ✍️

**Score two projects (90 minutes).**

Pick **two under-construction projects in the same micro-market**, ideally by different promoters.
Run the Developer Project Analysis Framework on both. Score A–F out of 5. Write a paragraph on
which you'd recommend and — importantly — **for which type of client**.

The insight you're hunting: the better project is often better *for a different buyer* than the one
in front of you.

## 8. Case study 📋

**The project that stopped filing.**

A Navi Mumbai project registered in 2021, completion declared December 2025. You check in mid-2026:

- Registration status: **active**, one extension granted to December 2026
- QPRs: filed through Q2 FY25, then **three consecutive quarters missing**
- Declared completion: **61%** — unchanged across the last two filings
- Complaints: **four** s.18 complaints filed in the last year
- Site visit: structure topped out, no finishing work visible, few workers

Your client wants this flat because it's ₹1,100/sq ft cheaper than anything comparable.

**Questions:** What is actually happening? What are the client's rights if they buy and it stalls?
Do you sell this?

<details><summary>Analysis</summary>

**What's happening.** Three missing QPRs plus flat declared progress plus visible site inactivity
is the standard signature of a project that has run out of money. The extension bought time, not
cash. The four s.18 complaints tell you existing allottees have already concluded the same thing.
The ₹1,100/sq ft discount is not a bargain — **it is the market pricing this risk**, and pricing it
roughly correctly.

**Rights if they buy and it stalls (s.18).** On failure to complete or give possession by the
agreed date, the allottee may either:
- **withdraw** — full refund of the amount paid **with interest** at the prescribed rate, plus compensation; or
- **stay** — and receive **interest for every month of delay** until possession.
Compensation is adjudicated by the **Adjudicating Officer** (s.71). Recovery is as **arrears of
land revenue** (s.40).

But understand what those rights are worth in practice: an order against a promoter with no money
is an order, not a payment. RERA gives an excellent remedy; it does not manufacture funds.

**Do you sell it?** No — not to a buyer who needs to live in it, is stretching to afford it, or
would be paying rent and EMI simultaneously. You could discuss it with a cash-rich investor who
understands they are underwriting a **completion risk** and might participate in an allottees'
association completing the project under **s.8**. That is a specialist bet, not a home purchase.

**What you actually do:** put the QPR gap, the flat progress and the four complaints in writing to
the client. Then let them decide with full information. That written note is both good service and
your own protection under **s.10(c)**.
</details>

## 9. Quiz — 10 questions

1. State the s.3(2)(a) exemption precisely. Is it "and" or "or"?
2. Do phases of a project register separately?
3. What must be in the separate account, and who certifies withdrawals?
4. Name the three accounts in the current Maharashtra structure and what each holds.
5. Within how long must the Authority grant or reject, and what happens if it doesn't?
6. On what ground and for how long is extension ordinarily granted?
7. Name the three grounds for revocation under s.7.
8. What may the Authority allow after revocation, under s.8?
9. What are Forms 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5, and who signs each?
10. What are the allottee's two options under s.18 when possession is delayed?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Land **not exceeding 500 sq m** **or** apartments **not exceeding 8**, inclusive of all phases. It is **"or"** — exceeding either threshold triggers registration.
2. **Yes** — each phase is a standalone real estate project requiring its own registration.
3. **70%** of amounts realised from allottees, for **land and construction cost only**; withdrawals certified by **Architect, Engineer and Chartered Accountant** in proportion to completion.
4. **Collection** (all receipts incl. parking/amenities, excl. taxes) · **Separate** (70%) · **Transaction** (up to 30%). Single scheduled bank, free of encumbrance.
5. **30 days**; if the Authority neither grants nor rejects, the project is **deemed registered**.
6. **Force majeure**, ordinarily **up to one year** — s.6.
7. Default under the Act/rules/regulations; violation of terms of approval by the competent authority; unfair practice or irregularities including false/misleading advertisement and fraud. Minimum **30 days' notice** and a hearing.
8. Allow the **association of allottees** to complete the project (after consulting the appropriate government), bar the promoter from the website and list them as a defaulter.
9. Form 1 **Architect** (% completion) · Form 2 **Engineer** (cost incurred) · Form 2A Quality Assurance · Form 3 **CA** (cost, withdrawal proportionality) · Form 4 **Architect** on completion · Form 5 **CA** annual statement of accounts.
10. **Withdraw** — refund with interest plus compensation; or **stay** — interest for every month of delay until possession.
</details>

## 12. One-page revision sheet

> **s.3** — no advertising/marketing/booking/selling without registration.
> **Exempt:** ≤500 sq m **or** ≤8 apartments · completed pre-Act · repair/redevelopment without marketing.
> **Each phase registers separately.**
>
> **s.4** — disclosure: track record, approvals, plans, **proformas**, carpet areas, encumbrances,
> **70% affidavit**. **s.5** — 30 days, else **deemed registered**.
>
> **Three accounts:** Collection (all) → Separate (**70%**) → Transaction (≤30%). One scheduled bank, unencumbered.
>
> **s.6** extension — force majeure, ordinarily **1 year**. **s.7** revocation — default / breach of
> approval / unfair practice; 30 days' notice. **s.8** — defaulter listing; allottees' association may complete.
>
> **Promoter duties:** 11 web page + QPR · 12 advert veracity · **13 the 10% rule** ·
> 14 sanctioned plans + **5-yr defect liability** · 15 no transfer without 2/3rds · 17 conveyance ·
> **18 refund with interest / monthly interest for delay**.
>
> **QPR:** 20 Jul · 20 Oct · 20 Jan · 20 Apr. **Forms:** 1 Architect · 2 Engineer · 3 CA · 4 Architect (completion) · 5 CA (annual).
>
> **Framework gates:** A Legal standing · F Client fit. Fail either and there is no price that fixes it.
