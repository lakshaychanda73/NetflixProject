# "What Should I Study Today?"

Open this when you have time but no plan. Find your slot, do exactly what it says, close the file.

**No decisions required.** Deciding what to study is the tax that stops people studying.

---

## ⏱ 30 MINUTES

**Do exactly one of these. Rotate through them in order — day 1 is A, day 2 is B, and so on.**

| | Task |
|---|---|
| **A** | Read the **Module 2 revision sheet** + the **sections table**. Cover the "Simple meaning" column and recite it. |
| **B** | Read the **Module 5 revision sheet**. Then write out **s.9, s.10 and s.62** from memory. Check. |
| **C** | Do **one module quiz** cold (10 questions). Score it. Re-read only what you got wrong. |
| **D** | Run **steps 1–5** of the MahaRERA investigation on any project. Just the first five. |
| **E** | Read **one MahaRERA order** from [Orders & Judgments](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/orders-judgements). One. In full. |
| **F** | Do the **Module 10 practice questions**. By hand, no calculator where possible. |
| **G** | Scan [orders](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/order) and [circulars](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/circular) for anything new. Note anything affecting agents. |
| **H** | Read the **penalty grid** in Module 7 §12. Redraw it from memory. |

> **If you only ever have 30 minutes:** cycle A → B → C → H forever. That alone will get you
> through the exam.

## 🕐 1 HOUR

**Pick the pairing that matches where you are.**

**Still learning the law:**
- 30 min — read one module's §2 (the explanation)
- 20 min — watch that module's #1 video
- 10 min — do the quiz

**Consolidating:**
- 20 min — two revision sheets
- 25 min — both those modules' quizzes, cold
- 15 min — re-read only your wrong answers

**Building skill:**
- 45 min — full **15-step investigation** on one project
- 15 min — write the five-line note

**Staying current:**
- 20 min — scan new orders and circulars
- 25 min — read one MahaRERA judgment in full
- 15 min — update your wrong-answer card and your micro-market notes

## 🕑 2 HOURS

**The standard study block. One module, properly.**

```
0:00–0:45   Read the module's explanation (§2) and its main table
0:45–1:15   Read the linked OFFICIAL source — the Act section, the rule, the order
1:15–1:40   Watch the module's top-priority video
1:40–1:55   Do the quiz, cold
1:55–2:00   Read the one-page revision sheet
```

**Module order if you're starting fresh:** 2 → 5 → 3 → 7 → 4 → 6 → 8 → 9 → 10 → 1 → 11 → 12 → 13

*(Yes, Module 1 comes late in this sequence. If you already work in real estate, its content is
mostly vocabulary you have. If you're new, do it first.)*

---

## 🕒 3 HOURS

**A module plus real practice. This is the block that actually builds competence.**

```
0:00–1:15   Module: read §2, the main table, and the official source
1:15–1:45   Watch the two top videos
1:45–2:45   ✍️ THE MODULE'S PRACTICAL EXERCISE — the whole thing, properly
2:45–3:00   Quiz + revision sheet
```

**Never skip the 1:45–2:45 block.** It is the only part that transfers to real work. Reading
Module 8 makes you informed; doing the due-diligence pack makes you useful.

## The five habits that matter more than any schedule

| Frequency | Habit |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | Convert one quoted price to **₹ per carpet sq ft**. Until it's automatic. |
| **Weekly** | Scan MahaRERA [orders](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/order) and [circulars](https://maharera.maharashtra.gov.in/circular). Five minutes. |
| **Monthly** | Read **one** MahaRERA order in full. |
| **Quarterly** | Refresh your micro-market numbers from the latest Knight Frank / ANAROCK / JLL. |
| **Annually** | Re-verify every **⏱** rate in the [time-sensitive register](master-resource-library.md#f--time-sensitive-register). |

## The one-question self-test

Before you close the course each day, answer this out loud:

> **"What did I learn today that would change what I'd say to a client tomorrow?"**

If the answer is nothing, you read but didn't study. Pick a 30-minute task from the list above and
do it properly.
