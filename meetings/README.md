# Meeting Notes

Structured notes from real estate / business meetings. One file per meeting.

**Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD-<counterparty>.md`

**Standard structure:**
1. Snapshot table (date, counterparty, status)
2. The Gist — whole meeting in ten lines
3. The Business — what they actually are
4. The Business Model — how money moves
5. Their own diagnosis
6. The Numbers — reconciled
7. **Fact-check** — claims verified against published sources, with verdicts
8. Concepts to study
9. Opportunities on the table
10. What this means for me
11. Next plan of action
12. Questions for next time
13. Open items & unverified claims
14. Sources

> **Convention:** what *they said* and what *I concluded* are always kept in separate sections. Every factual claim gets a ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌ verdict with a source link.
