# IIM Mumbai Alumni Committee — Task 1 Research Backup

Working notes behind the Benchmark Pack and the *Roll Call* proposal. Source numbers `[n]` match `Sources.md` and page 6 of the Benchmark Pack. Research cut-off: 8 October 2026.

**Method and limits**
- Official institute and alumni pages were used first. Media was used only where official pages were silent (IITD Alumni Day, IITB Alumni Day pledge, IIMC Clarion Call, IIMC Nostalgia 2019).
- The live institute sites and LinkedIn/Instagram pages could not be opened directly from the research environment. Facts were taken from search-indexed copies of those official pages.
- **Follower counts are indexed snapshots**, not live counts. The snapshot year is given where known. The "last 10 posts" engagement column is left `[add]` in the pack and must be filled from the live pages before submission.
- n/p = not publicly disclosed. Nothing is estimated unless marked *(est.)*.

## 6. Cross-institute analysis

**Common patterns**: a student-run interface (SARC, IIMC Alumni Cell, IIMB Student Alumni Committee); one flagship event plus a repeatable smaller format; 1:1 mentoring; awards.

**White space** (what nobody does):
1. No peer publishes what happens after a mentor match.
2. No peer runs a recurring career series in alumni's own words. The IIMB Podcast is institute-run and mixes faculty, students and alumni.
3. Phonathon-style contact reaches the most alumni at the lowest cost, but leaves no reusable output.
4. Mentoring asks are large (year-long 1:1); small fixed asks (admissions panels, Gravitas breakouts) are rare and work.

**IIM Mumbai-specific**: followers are split across two LinkedIn brands after the rename. The portal's 10,000+ figure is unqualified. Regional meets (Apr–Jun) and Sansmaran (Nov/Dec) already exist to welcome alumni back.

## 7. Roll Call — operating detail (for interview)

**Call script (6 questions, ~20 min)**
1. Where are you now (city, company, role, function)? Is the email we hold still right?
2. What was the one move between campus and today that mattered most, and why?
3. What do students misunderstand about your function?
4. What would you tell a first-year preparing for summers in your field?
5. Would you answer up to 2 screened student questions a month (15 min each)? Which topics?
6. Can we expect you at Sansmaran / your city's regional meet? Who from your batch should we call next?

**Form fields (8)**: city · company · role · function · batch/programme · preferred email · Ask Desk opt-in + topics · Sansmaran/regional intent. Consent is a checkbox for publishing a Path Card.

**Path Card template**: name, batch, current role → three career moves (one line each) → "What students get wrong" (≤40 words) → one piece of advice (≤25 words). The alumnus approves the text by WhatsApp or email before posting.

**Ask Desk rules**: students submit through a form with a specific question and context. The committee screens and routes. Cap of 2 asks per alumnus per month. Response target is 7 days. Phone numbers are never shared; the first contact goes through the committee.

**Pilot arithmetic** *(est.)*: 10 callers × ~2 completed calls/week × 5 calling weeks = 100 completed calls. A ~42% completion rate on 240 contacted is an assumption, to be tested in week 1. Path Cards: 4/week × 6 = 24.

**Budget** *(est.)*: Path Card wall print at Sansmaran ₹5,000; thank-you postcards ₹3,000. Total ~₹8,000. Calls use WhatsApp or personal minutes at no cost.

**Scale path**: after the pilot, run two seasons a year: Oct–Dec (feeds Sansmaran) and Mar–May (feeds regional meets and summers). First-years each make 3 calls during induction. Ask Desk opt-ins grow into IIMA-style functional circles once function data exists. Records sync to the alumni portal.
