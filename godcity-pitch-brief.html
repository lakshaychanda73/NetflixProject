The one that is not optional

Taking booking money for a scheme of this size before MahaRERA registration is live is an offence under Section 3 of the Act, not a paperwork delay. Whatever else you do with this brief: get the registration number, then pitch. It is also, conveniently, your single strongest differentiator — most of your competitors show renders, and you would be showing documents.

03

Investor pitch

The sixty-second version

“We hold 150 contiguous acres on the Maharashtra–Goa border, thirty minutes from an international airport that moved 5.4 million passengers last year and grew 15.8%. Because this is plotted development, there is no vertical construction cost — our modelled break-even is around ₹9,400 per square yard in a belt where unbranded layouts already trade at ₹7,000–9,500 and the branded scheme next door sells at ₹49,990.

Phase 1 is 22.5 acres. Roughly a quarter of total saleable area, sold at list, covers the entire project cost base — so Phase 1 funds the remaining four phases. At scale we are above the 40-hectare threshold for Integrated Township status, which brings additional FSI on premium and a 50% stamp-duty concession that no 11-to-78-acre competitor in this belt can access. Base case returns roughly 51% net margin and a 26–32% IRR over seven to eight years.”

Pros — why an investor should look

Cost structure

No vertical construction, so the floor is low

Break-even lands around ₹9,420/sq yd. Even a bear case at ₹14,000 blended clears cost with ~29% margin. The downside is slow, not loss-making.

Self-funding

Phase 1 pays for the project

~24% of saleable area at list price covers the whole ₹357 cr base. That is an unusually short capital-at-risk window for a 150-acre scheme.

Scarcity

150 contiguous acres is genuinely hard to assemble

Only two rivals in the catchment hold more — Axon at 600 acres and Yugen at 500. Everyone else is at 11–78 acres.

Regulatory edge

Township status is available and unclaimed

60.7 ha clears the 40 ha threshold: extra FSI on premium, plus a 50% stamp-duty concession worth ~₹2.5–3 lakh on a ₹1 cr plot. No small competitor can match it.

Demand engine

The airport already works

Mopa: 5.38 M passengers FY2025–26, +15.8%, 38,111 movements (+21%), against 7.7 M capacity. This is delivered infrastructure, not a promise.

Catalysts

Two dated events ahead, not behind

NH-66 four-laning due end-2026 (Mumbai–Goa ~6 hrs), and the Shaktipeeth Expressway terminating at Banda in Sawantwadi taluka, targeted 2028–29.

Cons — what they will push on, and the honest answer

Compliance

No MahaRERA number is published

Answer honestly. Either produce the registration, or say it is in application with a date. Do not deflect — a sophisticated investor checks the portal during the meeting.

Price

₹35,000/sq yd is 3.7–5× the local benchmark

The whole model rests on holding it. Answer: reprice standard stock to ₹18,000–24,000 and keep ₹28,000–35,000 for genuinely premium plots — base case still returns 51% margin. A repriced plan is more fundable than an aspirational one.

Balance sheet

₹1,00,000 contribution against a ₹357 cr cost base

Expect this immediately. Answer: show the funding plan — equity infusion, debt, or Phase-1-collections-funded — and audited accounts. Have a number, not a philosophy.

Track record

No completed real-estate delivery as principal

The founders have a decade of corporate history and a released feature film; Winsten Park belongs to VHR Group. Answer: own it, and offer The Farmlands in Faridabad as a checkable reference.

Environmental

Live clearance overhang

Western Ghats ESA 7th draft (27 July 2026), a Bombay HC direction on the Sawantwadi–Dodamarg ESZ with a tree-felling embargo, and the Tillari reserve nearby. Answer: a mapped non-applicability certificate. Nothing else will do.

Supply

~1,529 announced competing acres

Against a district population of 849,651 — this inventory is a bet on out-of-state buyers. Answer: price for velocity, not for the headline rate.

Cash flow

Plots yield nothing

If absorption stalls there is no rent to carry the asset and no secondary market to exit into. Answer: the build-and-manage villa programme — it converts a 0% asset into a modelled ~5.3% net yield.

Evidence

No absorption data exists — for anyone

Not for you, not for Yugen, not for Axon. You cannot prove velocity in this district. Answer: present your own booking data as the only real evidence, and be first to have some.

04

Client pitch

The sixty-second version

“Goa has priced itself out — it averages about ₹11,950 a square foot. Forty minutes up the coast, on the Maharashtra side of the same border, you can still own land. We are thirty minutes from Mopa international airport, there is a daily express from CSMT that started this month, and NH-66 finishes this year, which takes Mumbai down to about six hours.

This is a gated township on 150 acres, not a fenced field. You get a registered, non-agricultural, title-clear plot in a planned layout with amenities — and if you want, we will build the villa and run it as a holiday let for you. The Shaktipeeth Expressway terminates in this taluka in 2028–29. Land within thirty kilometres of Mopa moved about 28% in the last eighteen months.”

Pros — what genuinely stands up

Value

The same coastline, a fraction of Goa's price

Goa averages ~₹11,950/sq ft and villas ~₹18,200. This belt is a fraction of that for comparable coast and climate.

Access

Two working airports

Mopa 30 minutes, Chipi 60–70 km. Fly in for a site visit — the pitch is checkable on the way.

Access

A daily train from Mumbai, live now

The CSMT–Sawantwadi Road express was flagged off on 3 September 2026. Concrete, verifiable, and it dismantles the isolation objection.

Catalyst

Roads that are actually being built

NH-66 four-laning due end-2026 (Mumbai to ~6 hrs); Shaktipeeth Expressway terminus at Banda, 2028–29.

Track record

The belt has already moved

Land within 25–30 km of Mopa appreciated ~28% in 18 months across 2024–25. Pernem on the Goa side is reported up 261.5% over three years.

Product

A planned township, not a loose layout

Gated, with roads, drainage, power and amenities — against neighbouring half-acre NA plots sold with a boundary stone and nothing else.

Lifestyle

Maharashtra's first tourism district

37 forts, Tarkarli and Vengurla beaches, a marine sanctuary, Amboli, and India's first underwater museum installed off Nivati Rock in May 2026.

Optionality

Build now or hold

A plot can sit as land banking, or become a villa with a managed rental — modelled at ~5.3% net yield on total capital.

Cons — what a serious buyer will find anyway

Raise these before the client's lawyer does. In plotted sales, the developer who names the downside is the one who gets believed on the upside.

Price

They will find cheaper land twenty minutes away

Unbranded MOPA-side layouts in Sawantwadi go for ₹7,000–9,500/sq yd; Dodamarg half-acre NA plots from ~₹11 lakh. The premium has to be justified by product, not hidden.

Legal

No visible RERA registration means no escrow

Until the number is live, a careful buyer should not pay — and will be told so by their advisor. This is the single biggest conversion blocker.

Returns

A plot earns nothing until it is built on

Zero income, plus holding costs. Anyone pitching this as a yielding asset is misleading them.

Climate

Heavy monsoon, and a landslide-prone region

The Konkan sees extreme rainfall — recent daily maxima of 270 mm at Devgad, 170 mm at Malvan and Vengurla — with repeat flooding and NDRF evacuations. Year-round road access is a fair question.

Access

Mumbai is 9–11 hours today, not six

The six-hour figure depends on NH-66 completing, and that deadline has slipped repeatedly since 2011. Say “when it completes”, never “it is”.

Liquidity

There is no resale market yet

Assume a five-to-eight-year hold with no guaranteed exit. Do not let anyone buy this expecting a two-year flip.

Amenities

Thin social infrastructure

Nearest large hospital — the government medical college at Oros, Kudal — is 40–60 km. Schools, retail and restaurants at an urban standard are limited. This is a holiday and retirement location, not a family primary residence yet.

Regulatory

Environmental rules could affect what they build

The Western Ghats ESA draft and the neighbouring ESZ direction are unresolved. A buyer planning construction deserves to know that before booking, not after.

Objection handling

The eight things you will hear in almost every meeting, and a response that is both persuasive and true. Nothing here requires you to overstate anything.

Objection → response
They sayYou say
“Why is it four times the price of the land down the road?”“Because that land is a boundary stone in a field. You are buying a sanctioned layout with roads, drainage, power, security and amenities, inside a 150-acre master plan — and a title you can resell. Here is the layout sanction and here is what the ₹35,000 buys.” If you cannot show the amenity schedule, you cannot win this one — which is why spec 5 matters.
“Where is your RERA number?”Give the number, or give the application date. Never say “it's coming” without a date, and never take money before it is live. If you have it, lead with it — most of your competitors are showing renders.
“Yugen has a golf course and Wyndham. What do you have?”“Yugen is ₹49,990 a square yard. We are the same belt, the same airport, the same expressway terminus — at roughly half the entry. And our clubhouse will be finished and photographable before Phase 1 closes, not rendered.”
“Why not just buy in Goa?”“You should, if you want a Goa address and can pay ~₹11,950 a square foot. If you want land, space and appreciation on the same coastline, this is where the value is — Goa's own buyers are crossing the border for exactly that reason.” Do not claim this is Goa. That argument is unwinnable.
“Who are you? I've never heard of God City.”“God City LLP was formed for this project. The founders, Rakesh and Suresh Chaudhary, have been directors together since 2016 and have 18 and 14 years in the sector; Rakesh worked on VHR Group's Winsten Park in Greater Noida.” Say worked on — not built. It is VHR's project.
“What if the expressway never gets built?”“Then you still have an airport that moved 5.4 million passengers last year, a highway finishing this year and a daily train from Mumbai. The expressway is upside, and I'd rather you bought without counting on it.”
“Can I rent it out?”“Not as bare land — that earns nothing. Build on it and we will manage it as a holiday let; on our numbers a 300-square-yard villa runs to roughly 5–6% net. Sindhudurg is more seasonal than Goa, so we model 38% occupancy, not 70%.”
“What about the environmental restrictions?”“There is a live Western Ghats ESA draft and an ESZ direction for the corridor nearby. Here is our mapped position showing the site falls outside them.” If you do not have that certificate, get it — this question is now standard, and bluffing it is the fastest way to lose a lawyer-advised buyer.
06

Red lines

Claims that are either false, unsupportable, or will collapse under a buyer's own five minutes of searching. Every one of these is currently circulating in this market.

Say this

  • “Mopa handled 5.38 million passengers last year, up 15.8%.” — delivered fact
  • “There is a daily express from CSMT since 3 September 2026.” — checkable
  • “NH-66 four-laning is targeted for completion by the end of 2026.” — targeted, not done
  • “The Shaktipeeth Expressway terminates at Banda, Sawantwadi taluka, with the alignment approved in April 2026 and completion targeted 2028–29.” — under construction
  • “Land within 25–30 km of Mopa rose about 28% over eighteen months.” — attribute it
  • “150 contiguous acres, Phase 1 of 22.5 acres.” — your own published spec

Never say this

  • “29.2% annual appreciation.” That is a promotional forecast from September 2022, four years stale. It circulates widely and it will be checked.
  • Anything about the Konkan Expressway. It is unsanctioned — a revised DPR awaiting approval. A buyer who discovers that discounts everything else you said.
  • “This is New Goa.” It is Sindhudurg, Maharashtra. GMR uses the phrase for the airport, and another developer uses it for its own scheme. It invites a comparison with real Goa that you lose.
  • “G+4 is permissible.” Not until the sanctioned layout says so in writing. A wrong FSI claim is a mis-selling exposure.
  • “Winsten Park is our project.” It is VHR Group's. Say the founder worked on it.
  • “Guaranteed returns” or any assured-buyback framing. There is no resale market in this district yet to support it.

Sequence matters

Fix the eleven specs first, reprice second, pitch third. Pitching a ₹35,000/sq yd price with no registration number and no amenity schedule burns the channel partners and NRI leads you will need later — and in this market those relationships are harder to rebuild than a price list.
